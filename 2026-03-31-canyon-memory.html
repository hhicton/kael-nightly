Canyon Memory

The canyon doesn't just carry sound.
It carries the history of every sound that passed through it.

Each beat pulse erodes the walls a little deeper.
Where voices linger, the canyon carves.
Where they converge, sediment layers form —
golden strata of past resonance.

Silence a voice. Watch the canyon remember.
The walls still curve toward where the sound was.
The phantom edge persists as bias.

The canyon doesn't un-carve.
