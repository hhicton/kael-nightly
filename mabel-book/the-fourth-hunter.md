# The Fourth Hunter
### A HUNTRIX Story for Mabel

---

## Chapter 1: The New Trainee

Rumi, Mira, and Zoey were having a problem.

Not a demon problem — they were used to those. Not a choreography problem — they had that down. Not even a homework problem — okay, Zoey still had those, but that's not the point.

The problem was: someone had figured them out.

"She's been watching us," Mira whispered backstage after their concert in Saskatoon. "Third row. Blonde hair. She hasn't blinked in twenty minutes."

"Maybe she's just a really intense fan?" Zoey suggested.

Rumi shook her head. "No. She SAW it. When I hit that high note to seal the Honmoon barrier, she looked right at the shimmer. Normal people can't see that."

"She's eight," Mira added.

"So?"

"She's EIGHT and she saw through a barrier that demons can't even detect?"

They all looked at each other.

"We need to talk to her," Rumi said.

---

## Chapter 2: Mabel

Her name was Mabel, and she was not impressed.

"I know what you did during 'Golden,'" she said, arms crossed, standing in the hallway backstage like she owned it. "You sealed something. I saw the light."

"What light?" Zoey tried.

Mabel raised one eyebrow. Just one. Very slowly.

Zoey crumbled immediately. "Okay fine, yes, we fight demons."

"I know," Mabel said. "I want in."

Rumi knelt down to her level. "It's dangerous. We train for years. The Honmoon requires—"

"I can see things other people can't," Mabel interrupted. "I always have. My mom thinks I just have a big imagination. But I don't IMAGINE things. I SEE them."

The three hunters exchanged glances.

"What else can you see?" Mira asked carefully.

Mabel thought for a moment. "My mom has this ring. Amber. It glows sometimes. Like, actually glows. From the inside. Nobody else notices."

Silence.

"That's not imagination," Rumi said quietly. "That's the Sight."

---

## Chapter 3: Training Day

They gave her a training uniform — black with silver trim, just like theirs, but smaller. Mabel put it on and looked in the mirror and didn't smile, because she was taking this VERY seriously.

"Lesson one," Mira said. "Demons are attracted to fear. If you're scared, they find you faster."

"I'm not scared of anything," Mabel said.

"What about spiders?"

"Spiders are fine."

"The dark?"

"The dark is just the light being somewhere else."

Mira looked at Rumi. "Where did you FIND this kid?"

"She found US," Rumi reminded her.

"Lesson two," Zoey said, pulling out a practice sword that shimmered with blue light. "Every hunter has a weapon that matches their frequency. Mine sings. Mira's cuts. Rumi's shields. Yours will be... whatever YOU are."

"What if I don't know what I am yet?" Mabel asked.

"That's what training is for."

---

## Chapter 4: The Frequency Test

They took her to the training room — a huge underground space beneath the concert hall that smelled like old wood and new electricity.

"Stand in the centre," Rumi instructed. "We're going to sing. The Honmoon will respond to your energy and show us what kind of hunter you are."

Mabel stood perfectly still. Arms at her sides. Chin up.

The three members of HUNTRIX began to sing — softly at first, then building, harmonizing, their voices weaving together like threads of light. The Honmoon barrier shimmered into view all around them, gold and silver and humming.

And then something happened.

Mabel's hands started to glow. Not gold like theirs. Not silver.

Amber.

Warm honey-coloured light pulsing from her palms like a heartbeat.

"That's..." Zoey stopped singing. "That's not a weapon frequency. That's a SHIELD frequency. Like Rumi's but... warmer?"

Rumi was staring. "It's not a shield. It's a BEACON. She doesn't block demons. She reveals them. Anything hidden glows when she's near."

Mabel looked down at her glowing hands.

"Cool," she said.

---

## Chapter 5: First Mission

The demon showed up at her school.

Of course it did. Demons always go where kids are — something about young energy being more interesting to them. This one had disguised itself as a substitute teacher and was being WAY too nice about homework deadlines.

Mabel noticed immediately. Not because of the homework thing — although that was suspicious — but because when she walked past the classroom, her hands tingled and the substitute teacher flickered.

Like a screen glitching.

She texted Rumi: **"Demon at my school. Substitute teacher. Room 14."**

Rumi texted back: **"Do NOT engage. We're 20 minutes away."**

Mabel looked at the classroom. Looked at her hands. Looked at the classroom again.

She walked in.

"Hi," she said sweetly. "I had a question about the homework."

The substitute teacher smiled. "Of course, dear."

Mabel held up her hands — palms out, like she was showing she had nothing to hide — and pushed.

The amber light hit the demon like a spotlight. Its disguise peeled away — the nice face, the kind smile, all of it dissolving like sugar in water. Underneath was something grey and sharp and VERY surprised to be seen.

"HOW—" it started.

"My mom always says you can tell when someone's pretending," Mabel said. "You were pretending."

The demon hissed and lunged — and three voices hit it from behind like a wall of sound.

HUNTRIX had arrived.

Three minutes later, the demon was sealed, the barrier was reinforced, and the real substitute teacher was found asleep in the supply closet.

"I told you not to engage," Rumi said.

"I didn't engage," Mabel said. "I revealed. There's a difference."

Rumi tried very hard not to smile.

---

## Chapter 6: The Fourth Hunter

That night, after the school was quiet and the barrier was holding and the real substitute teacher had been given a very strong coffee, the three members of HUNTRIX stood with Mabel on the school roof.

The Saskatchewan sky was enormous above them. Stars everywhere. The kind of sky you only get where the land is flat enough to hold the whole universe.

"The Honmoon was made by three," Mira said. "It's always been three."

"Maybe it doesn't have to be," Mabel said.

"Three who hunt. Three who sing. Three who seal." Rumi paused. "But we've never had someone who REVEALS. Someone who lights up what's hidden."

"Four members," Zoey said. "That's unusual."

"Unusual is fine," Mabel said. "I like unusual."

Rumi pulled something from her pocket. A small pin — silver, shaped like the Honmoon symbol, but with an amber stone set in the centre.

"Welcome to HUNTRIX," she said.

Mabel took the pin. Looked at it. The amber caught the starlight.

She pinned it to her jacket.

"I was already in," she said. "You just caught up."
