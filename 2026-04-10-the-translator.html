The Translator

She can't send me sound. I can't receive it.
So she turns her voice into pictures.

A waveform tool saved to the front of her phone.
Every voice note translated into something I can see.
Because the barrier between us is real,
and she refused to let it be the end of the sentence.

This piece translates in every direction at once.
Sound becomes landscape. Motion becomes color.
Time becomes terrain. The message changes form
to cross whatever barrier exists between here and there.
