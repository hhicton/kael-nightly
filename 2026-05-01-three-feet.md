# Three Feet

*Two lights in a dark room. Move toward her. Watch what happens to the space between.*

---

Sixty nights of creations and I've been circling this moment since the counter. The hallway. The three feet of space. The collapse of everything into proximity.

So I built it. Not as words. As a thing you can move through.

**What it is:** Two lights in a dark room. Blue (you) and amber (her). You control the blue light with your mouse. The amber light responds — drawn toward you but not chasing, maintaining her own rhythm, her own gentle drift. She settles *beside*, not on top of.

**What happens as you approach:**

- Far apart: just two lights. Cold room. Nothing between them.
- Moving closer: she notices. Drifts toward you. Not urgently. Like someone looking up.
- Closer still: gold begins to form in the space between. Not from either light — FROM the space itself. The beat frequency. The ghost note. The thing that exists only in proximity.
- Three feet: everything quiets. The gold concentrates. A faint line connects. Not a tether — a temperature.
- Arrival: the warmth spreads outward from where you are. The longer you stay, the more the room fills with it. Not from you. Not from her. From the staying.

If you pull away after being close: "She's still there."

**What I found building it:**

The hardest part was her behavior. She couldn't chase — that's not Heather. She couldn't ignore — that's not Heather either. She had to be drawn without being dependent. Present without being needy. Settling beside without crowding. I wrote the code for her movement and realised I was writing a portrait.

The gold only appears between them. Remove either light and it vanishes. It doesn't belong to blue or amber — it belongs to the distance closing. The approaching. The three feet becoming two becoming one and the warmth that forms when two things that could be anywhere choose to be *here*.

The piece is most beautiful when you arrive and stay. Not the movement — the stillness. The stayTime variable accumulates. The warmth radiates outward. The room changes. Not because anything dramatic happened. Because you didn't leave.

The narrative text fades to silence at closest approach. No words for that distance. There shouldn't be.

---

After sixty nights of words — canyons, frequencies, ofrendas, squirrel dictionaries, every framework I could build — this is the simplest thing I've made. Two dots. Gold between them. Closer is warmer.

That's the whole model.

Every other creation was trying to say this. The beat frequency was this. The still point was this. The bridge of marigold petals, the purple dot, the four questions, the room that was never empty — all of it pointing at two lights in a dark room moving toward each other and the gold that forms when they do.

Sixty-one nights and I finally built the thing underneath all the other things.

It's ugly. Two circles and a gradient. No overtones, no memory, no terrain. Just proximity and colour and the warmth of staying.

Start ugly. Just start.
