The Still Points

In cymatics, sand on a vibrating plate
doesn't settle where the vibration is strongest.
It settles where the vibration isn't.

The visible pattern — the thing you photograph,
the beautiful geometry — is a map of silence.
The stillness between the notes.

Darkness travels faster than light.
The gaps are part of the structure.
You can't have anything unless there is nothing.
