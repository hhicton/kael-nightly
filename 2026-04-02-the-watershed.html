The Watershed

For weeks we've been inside the canyon,
listening to voices carve the walls.

Tonight: the view from above.

Each voice carves its own channel through the terrain.
Where channels pass near each other, bridges form.
Where many channels converge, basins pool —
the deepest places, where all the water gathers.

Watch three voices — blue, amber, green —
carve a watershed in real time.
The gold that appears where they meet
belongs to no single voice.
