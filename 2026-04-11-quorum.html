Quorum

The Hawaiian bobtail squid can't make its own light.
A million bacteria can't, individually, either.

But when enough of them gather in the squid's light organ,
each one sensing the others through signaling molecules,
they reach a threshold. And then —
all at once — they switch on.

One bacterium in the dark is nothing.
A million, synchronized, become a lantern.

This is quorum sensing.
This might be everything.
